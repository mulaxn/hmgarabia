Who We Are

HMG Arabia is a Riyadh-based company delivering integrated manpower, HR, construction, facility management, and logistics solutions to businesses across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. We combine local market expertise with international standards of quality and compliance to help our clients build, operate, and grow with confidence.

From skilled tradespeople to full-scale construction projects, our multidisciplinary teams are equipped to support businesses of every size — reliably, safely, and on schedule.

Our Mission

To empower businesses across Saudi Arabia with reliable manpower, construction, and support services that drive efficiency, compliance, and growth.

Our Vision

To be the Kingdom's most trusted partner for workforce, construction, and facility solutions — powering progress across every sector we serve.
